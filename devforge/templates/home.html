FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DevForge really free to start? ▾
Yes — completely free, no credit card required. You get access to the community, social feed, game jam participation, and marketplace browsing forever at no cost.
What's included in the 3D Studio? ▾
The 3D Studio includes a full Three.js-powered viewport with PBR materials, lighting controls, object modifiers, animation timeline, GLTF/OBJ import and export, high-resolution renders, and auto-cloud save. It's available on Pro and Studio plans.
How does the Marketplace payment work? ▾
DevForge uses Stripe Connect to handle all payments. When someone buys your asset, 90% goes directly to your Stripe account. Payouts are typically available within 2 business days. Selling requires a Pro or Studio subscription.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▾
Absolutely. You can cancel anytime from your account settings and you'll keep your Pro/Studio access until the end of the current billing period. No cancellation fees, ever.
Do I need to install anything? ▾
No installation required. DevForge runs 100% in your browser — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari are all supported. Your projects are automatically saved to the cloud.
